Diabetes Care & Tests

Everything your diabetes needs, under one roof

From the first sugar test to complication screening, the centre brings the investigations people with diabetes need into a single, familiar place.

Signature Tests

Catch complications early

The tests that make the biggest difference to long-term outcomes in diabetes.

CGMS

Continuous glucose monitoring

A small sensor on the arm records your sugar round the clock for up to two weeks — showing exactly how food, sleep and stress affect you.

Biothesiometer

Diabetic neuropathy check

A quick, painless vibration test of the feet that picks up early nerve damage, long before numbness or ulcers appear.

Risk Profiler

Diabetes risk profile

A complete vascular risk assessment that helps Dr. Chile make informed treatment decisions for your heart and blood vessels.

ABI & vascular age

Circulation in the legs

Ankle-brachial index and vascular-age testing check for narrowed arteries, a common and silent complication of diabetes.

Complication Screening

Protecting your nerves, eyes, liver & heart

Diabetes damages quietly. Regular screening finds problems while they can still be prevented or treated.

Neuropathy — Biothesiometer

Measures vibration sense in the feet to detect and track diabetic nerve damage objectively.

On advice

Diabetic retinopathy screening

Eye screening to catch changes in the retina early, before vision is affected.

Pre-arranged dates

FibroScan — fatty liver

A painless scan that measures liver fat and stiffness; fatty liver is common in Type 2 diabetes.

2nd & 4th Friday

Diabetes Risk Profiler

Vascular age and ankle-brachial index to assess risk to the heart and blood vessels.

On advice

ECG

A quick heart tracing — essential for people with diabetes, who can have silent heart disease.

24 × 7

2D Echo, colour Doppler & TMT
