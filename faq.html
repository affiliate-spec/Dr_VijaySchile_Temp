About diabetes reversal

Can Type 2 diabetes really be reversed?

For many people, yes. When weight, diet, activity, sleep and stress are addressed and the changes are kept up, blood sugar can return to a healthy range and medicines can be reduced. When HbA1c stays below 6.5% for at least three months without diabetes medicines, doctors call it remission. How far each person gets depends on how long they have had diabetes, their weight and how consistently the plan is followed.

Will I be able to stop my medicines?

Some patients come off medicines completely; many need fewer or lower doses. Medicines are reduced step by step, only when your sugar readings show it is safe. Please never stop or change a medicine on your own — sudden changes can be dangerous.

Can Type 1 diabetes be reversed?

No. Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the body cannot make enough insulin, so insulin is always needed. Healthy habits still make Type 1 much easier to control. Dr. Chile treats Type 1 diabetes in patients above 12 years.

Do I need to follow a special or expensive diet?

No. Plans are built around ordinary Indian home food — with changes to portions, timing and choices, and cutting out hidden sugars and ultra-processed foods. Dr. Chile's book and videos explain these in detail.

Tests at the centre

What is CGMS?

A Continuous Glucose Monitoring System is a small sensor worn on the upper arm that measures your sugar every few minutes for up to two weeks. It shows how meals, exercise, sleep and stress affect your sugar, which helps fine-tune your plan and medicines.

What is a biothesiometer test?

It is a quick, painless test that measures how well you feel vibration in your feet. It detects diabetic nerve damage (neuropathy) early and tracks it over time, so foot problems can be prevented.

When are FibroScan and eye screening done?

FibroScan for fatty liver is done on the 2nd and 4th Friday of each month. Diabetic retinopathy (eye) screening is done on pre-arranged dates — please call the centre to know the next date.

Do I need to fast before my tests?

Some tests, such as fasting sugar and lipid profile, need fasting, usually overnight. When you book, the team will tell you exactly how to prepare. Blood sugar testing is available 24×7.
