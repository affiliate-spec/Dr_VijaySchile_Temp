The Approach

Most people are told to manage diabetes for life. We aim higher.

Type 2 diabetes is driven largely by how we eat, move, sleep and handle stress. Change those — with the right tests and close follow-up — and for many people sugars fall, tablets can be reduced under supervision, and the disease goes into remission.

  • A plan built on Indian food habits, not a foreign diet chart
  • Continuous glucose monitoring to see what really moves your sugar
  • Medicines reduced only when your numbers say it is safe

Tests Under One Roof

See what your diabetes is really doing

Sugar readings tell only part of the story. The centre has the tests that catch complications early — in your nerves, blood vessels, eyes, liver and heart.

CGMS

Continuous glucose monitoring

A small sensor on the arm records your sugar round the clock for up to two weeks — showing exactly how food, sleep and stress affect you.

Biothesiometer

Diabetic neuropathy check

A quick, painless vibration test of the feet that picks up early nerve damage, long before numbness or ulcers appear.

Risk Profiler

Diabetes risk profile

A complete vascular risk assessment that helps Dr. Chile make informed treatment decisions for your heart and blood vessels.

ABI & vascular age

Circulation in the legs

Ankle-brachial index and vascular-age testing check for narrowed arteries, a common and silent complication of diabetes.
